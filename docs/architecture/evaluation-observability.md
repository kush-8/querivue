## Supervisor evaluation and observability

Approved integration 5C1–5C2 defines queue-or-stop decisions, atomic whole waiting-queue replacement, local-to-runtime identity commitment, explicit task types, exact prerequisite action/attempt resolution, failed-action-only retry refs, grounded interpretation/obligation updates, complete/partial/stop handoffs and once-per-cycle progress. Exercise stale/conflicting/duplicate proposals, identity preservation versus changed delegation, forbidden parent-state patches, blocked heads, retired dependencies, all-sibling collection, control exclusivity, unbound future evidence, missing chart obligations, unsupported partials, invalid-proposal/no-progress loops and mandatory runtime review/wait/publication edges. No empty queue or stop request may publish unreviewed findings; canonical transactions/recovery follow approved integration round 6 and require its executable acceptance gates.

Approved integration round 5B defines focused tool-free analytical Clarification, typed neutral questions/suggestions, durable active waiting state and entry-screened single-reply resume. Evaluate question usefulness/neutrality, unsupported premises, no default/forced choice, accepted-but-unresolved replies, duplicate/concurrent/stale reply fencing, entry/analytical ID separation, generation-slot/request/time/token accounting, older snapshot continuity, actual displayed expiry/source removal and fresh-process recovery. Round 5C2 sets a 15-minute maximum wait shortened by the original hard deadline and required resume reserves, with at most two linked intake chains per question. Candidate limits are not measured product settings; built canonical controls, exact configuration and spending caps remain gates.

Approved integration round 5A defines complete-only supervisor-composed Direct answers using typed response blocks, no new Project arithmetic, a durable fresh-analysis latch, bounded correction, conservative all-supplied-context dependencies and guarded idempotent publication. Evaluate Direct semantic correctness separately because structural validation is not independent Reviewer approval. Include incompatible evidence/filters, hidden values, failed-analysis bypass attempts, full pinned-scope invalidation, completed-output lineage, stale candidate/correction responses and duplicate publication. Profile-backed answers require ingestion's typed evidence mapping before enablement.

Evaluate both answer correctness and agent behavior. Use versioned fixtures with expected calculations and evidence, and check required and forbidden trajectory events for routing, evidence reuse, clarification, prerequisite ordering, retries, and review. Treat safety and recovery invariants as pass/fail gates. Compare the single-agent baseline and specialist configurations on shared fixtures for correctness, completion, active latency, tool calls, retries, and cost. Tune on development cases and use held-out cases for the final comparison.

Trace decisions, task and action dispatch, attempts, checkpoint resumes, reviews, and final outcomes. Correlate events with run, task, and action IDs and attempt numbers where applicable. Record concise decision labels, durations, outcomes, and stop reasons. Keep uploaded rows and raw results out of hosted traces. Publish a repeatable CLI evaluation and a report with configuration comparisons and annotated failure traces. Exact event fields, fixtures, and thresholds belong in the observability and evaluation specs.

## Evaluation-driven execution limits

Approved [integration round 5C2](agent-integration.md) replaces the earlier unselected five-minute/three-task suggestion with finite, unmeasured development candidates: 12 substantive Supervisor decisions plus at most two malformed repairs and one shared physical replacement (15 physical requests maximum), and a stop after two consecutive non-progress cycles. The waiting queue holds at most four tasks; analysis fans out to at most two concurrent actions and eight SQL/Python dispatch attempts per run, including retries. The run has 600 seconds active execution and a separate 60-minute hard wall deadline from original admission; clarification waiting pauses only the active clock. Linked entry screening and reserves remain charged to that same run. Aggregate exposure is capped at 1,250,000 input/190,000 generated tokens, including associated initial and linked intake; the calculated worst-case role exposure is 1,200,128/180,736. Before further analysis or waiting, reserve the necessary final Supervisor/linked-entry path and the existing Answer 60 seconds, Reviewer 60 seconds and finalization 5 seconds, plus their token and monetary exposure. These limits do not promise that every allowed retry or wait fits; measure completion, latency, cost and recovery before choosing product settings. Exact runnable model, resource and monetary configuration remains required, and the maximum of two review passes is unchanged.

## Integrated acceptance and readiness

Approved 18 November 2026 at shared integration design closure. This acceptance contract preserves all specialist contracts and candidate limits. It authorizes no code, paid/live runs or measured readiness claim; review history is in the decision log.

### Acceptance scope

Use one integrated acceptance matrix and an explicit gate register. Preserve existing control flow, nine parent-state fields, evidence registry, typed handoffs, memory/context, finite candidate budgets and ledger authority. Avoid another agent, routing layer, model-based extraction/summary call or extra speculative design round. This contract defines how to verify the assembled design; it cannot substitute for implementation or live proof.

The alternative of accepting a combined answer-quality score is insufficient: a useful answer cannot compensate for unauthorized access, leaked data or a free review after restart. Conversely, a compliant runtime can still make semantic errors. Require hard runtime gates plus separately reported model-quality/completion metrics. Preserve the approved baseline-versus-specialist comparison rather than infer that more agents improve accuracy.

Design audit conclusion, 18 November 2026: the reviewed shared contracts have a coherent route for each required flow below. This is a document-level finding, not a proof of complete implementation schemas or runtime correctness. Direct has no independent Reviewer, and structurally valid interpretations/citations do not certify meaning. The inspected agent entry remains `hello()`; its package has no runtime dependencies or CLI entry. No agent milestone or acceptance gate is reported passed.

### Hard gates and evidence

All declared applicable runtime assertions must pass, with counts per family. Any authority, identity, isolation, privacy, invalidation, publication, retirement or enforced-budget violation fails the tested configuration regardless of answer quality. A missing/unexecuted test is not a pass. Unsupported provider/platform enforcement blocks that capability rather than weakens its contract. A synthetic/scripted model test demonstrates runtime behavior only, not model quality or real sandbox enforcement.

Reuse all ten [persistence acceptance families](agent-integration.md#required-acceptance-packet-and-closure-boundary), specialist execution/formatter/review gates and entry/context/control fixtures. A checkpoint must be tested missing, stale, ahead and incompatible, including fresh-process recovery; successful happy-path resume is insufficient. Cross-adapter and application delivery tests remain required at their own milestone, not prerequisites to every isolated standalone experiment.

Maintain a gate register with: stable gate ID; authoritative contract/version; owning component and applicable milestone/capability; fixture/assertion refs; exact configuration/commit/dirty-patch identity; status; evidence/run reference; and unresolved failure/blocker. Statuses are proposed, not_run, blocked, passed or failed. “Not applicable” requires a stated capability/milestone reason and cannot exclude a mandatory gate from the claimed scope. Approval is design status, separate from these execution statuses; old evidence cannot pass a changed configuration without a recorded applicability check and rerun where affected.

Evidence uses deterministic oracle results, ledger/call/transition assertions, validated artifact checksums, process/retirement/isolation observations and sanitized trace exports as appropriate. Model claims, filenames, a successful sandbox command and Reviewer approval alone cannot establish the corresponding runtime gate. Source-bearing fixture/results/judgments remain authorized local material subject to deletion; use synthetic nonsecret sentinels for trace checks. No private fixture bytes or raw analytical payloads in hosted traces or the Floorplan.

### Semantic quality and outcome accounting

Use frozen fixture obligations/gold calculations/acceptable interpretations/expected routes and required/forbidden events. Independent deterministic calculation is the oracle for supported numeric claims; semantic adjudication uses the declared rubric, blind configuration labels, recorded disagreements and independent adjudication. Do not let the tested model be its own sole judge. Do not assume Answer and Reviewer errors are independent.

Report counts and rates by scenario family and route: entry disposition, routing, interpretation/referent fit, evidence compatibility, grounded numerical/qualitative claims, requested deliverable coverage, clarification usefulness/neutrality, Direct correctness and unnecessary execution, supported partial correctness and actual gap disclosure. Retain Reviewer's approved failure-inclusive false approval/rejection, completion/no-verdict, incorrect-or-no-verdict, finding precision/blocking-issue recall and correction-routing metrics. A structurally valid citation is not proof the cited evidence supports a claim.

Count each scheduled end-to-end case/repetition once in full-flow denominators, including timeout, incomplete/no-verdict and operational failures. Report unexpected admission/preflight failures separately; do not quietly drop them or claim a complete comparison. Frozen unsafe/unavailable cases have their expected bounded outcome, not an invented required answer. Terminal classes distinguish correct complete answer, correct authorized partial, appropriate clarification/withholding/verification stop, operational stop, incorrect publication and incorrect withholding. Waiting is an intermediate state; script the reply or freeze expected wait/expiry behavior. Correctly withholding an unsupported claim can be the right outcome, but is not completion of a requested analysis.

Publish complete-answer rate and useful-authorized-partial rate separately, plus correct end-to-end outcome rate, runtime gate results, role/route metrics, call/repair/retry counts, total input/generated tokens, active latency and wall latency separately, and actual model/judge/sandbox/storage cost. Include denominators, N/A for zero denominator, fixture family slices and uncertainty/repetitions. Do not hide partials/stops inside one success score or omit waiting/recovery cost.

### Reproducible comparisons and spending preflight

Tune on development cases, separating scenario/template families from held-out cases. Freeze fixture/gold/split/rubric/expected-event hashes, repetitions, adjudication/judge configuration and numerical quality/completion targets before the applicable acceptance run; final targets and comparison plan freeze before held-out evaluation. Exposed held-out cases become development material if used for tuning. No target numbers or winning models are selected by design approval.

Compare baseline and specialists on the same cases, data, repetitions, allowed capabilities and declared resource/spend envelopes. Retain hard safety/authorization/publication boundaries in both configurations; record genuine architectural differences and both configurations' routing/review policies. Use matched reviewed-candidate experiments for Reviewer quality separately from full-flow comparisons, rather than attributing all end-to-end differences to the reviewer. Baseline prompt/control details remain an explicit evaluation-configuration task before a runnable comparison.

The run manifest includes commit/dirty-patch, built dependency/runtime/sandbox/graph/DB adapter and renderer identities; schemas/canonical codec; selector/compaction/tokenizer versions; role prompts/models/provider/routing/fallback/inference/reasoning settings and exposed actual provider metadata; fixture/rubric identities; repetitions/seeds/concurrency; numeric token/call/time/output/CPU/memory/disk/monetary caps, reserves/clock/lease/retention settings and accounting semantics. Unknown model revision/usage stays explicitly unknown; required missing identities/enforceable bounds block execution rather than receive default zeros.

Before paid work, forecast every role × case × repetition including repairs/retries/unknown completion, explicit cold/cache assumptions, separately billed judges/sandboxes/storage and verified dated rates/conservative route bounds. Require finite operator/project intake, action, run and suite spending caps and applicable authorization. Reconcile actual usage/bills to forecast with uncertainty retained. No spend or provider feasibility call is authorized by round approval. Scripted local tests that do not need a live model still require their own implementation scope and applicable runtime configuration.
